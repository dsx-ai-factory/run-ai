Nir: change the weight budget

A stronger imbalance signal gives distribution scorers more weight.

Smoothed signalDistributionAffinity

g = 1 / (1 + exp(-6 × (I - 0.6)))
Distribution = 0.2 + 0.8 × g
Affinity = 1 - Distribution

What changes: weights within the configured profiles, during background updates.
